# AGROMAT Studio design brief

Build a readable analytics workspace for products, competitor prices, promotions and sales. Use a light canvas, white surfaces, subtle borders, restrained violet accents and clear typographic hierarchy. Keep navigation compact and content spacious. Use consistent spacing, rounded controls and visible focus states.

Preserve the established layout and interactions: searchable dropdown filters, removable filter chips, period controls, clear active states, pagination and detail views. Every clickable element must have a meaningful action. Maintain filter context when switching views where applicable. Support keyboard navigation and narrow screens.

Use smooth, shape-preserving chart curves. Compare actual periods year over year with clearly distinguished series and explicit dates. Never fabricate missing history. Label metric units, source freshness and aggregation scope. Separate attributed ecommerce events from completed shipments. Show unavailable data explicitly.

Prioritize readable numbers, concise Ukrainian labels and useful drilldowns. Avoid decorative complexity. Keep credentials and customer information out of the public interface.
